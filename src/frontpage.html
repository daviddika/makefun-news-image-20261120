The Big Story — Shenzhen, China

Three Leaders, One “Peace Declaration”

Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping meet for the first time as a trio — 90 minutes at a lakeside villa in Shenzhen — and issue a four-point “Declaration toward Peace”: a nuclear war “cannot be won and must never be fought”, more fuel for all nations, and reform of the United Nations.

The declaration says nothing about the wars in Ukraine and Iran. Trump calls the summit “bigger than Yalta” and flies home for a noon press conference.

“A nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.” — the joint declaration
War in Ukraine — Kyiv

Kyiv Hit by Ballistic Missiles

Russia strikes Kyiv overnight with about a dozen ballistic missiles; explosions and fires are reported across several districts. It follows a drone attack the day before that killed one person and damaged historic buildings and embassies.

Weather — India

Cyclone Akvan Makes Landfall

Severe Cyclone Akvan crosses the Odisha coast near Gopalpur, winds 90–100 km/h, gusting to 110. More than 300,000 people evacuated, 69 trains cancelled, red alert for heavy rain across Odisha and Andhra Pradesh.

Middle East — The Gulf

Gulf War at Sea Widens

Houthis claim drone and missile strikes on four Saudi targets; a tanker hits a mine east of Hormuz and catches fire. The IEA calls the Middle East conflict the biggest energy security threat in history.

Climate — Antalya, Türkiye

COP31 Closes, Deal in Hand

The UN climate conference closes today with a 144-paragraph draft and $120 billion on the table — but the latest text drops the reference to fossil fuels, leaving two options open to the final hour.
